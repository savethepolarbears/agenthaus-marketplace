# Analyze Keyword Metrics

Use this command to get comprehensive keyword performance data including search volume, cost-per-click (CPC), keyword difficulty, and competition analysis.

## Usage

```
/textfocus:keyword "your keyword" [--lang en]
```

## Parameters

- **keyword** (required): The keyword phrase to analyze
- **--lang** (optional): Language code (default: en). Examples: en, de, fr, es, it, nl, pt, ru, zh, ja

## What You Get

### Search Volume
- **Monthly searches**: Estimated monthly search volume globally or in specific markets
- **Trend data**: Whether the keyword is growing, stable, or declining
- **Seasonality**: Best months for content focused on this keyword

### Cost-Per-Click (CPC)
- **Advertiser spend**: Average amount advertisers pay per click (indicates commercial intent)
- **Value range**: Low (< $1), Medium ($1-5), High (> $5)
- **Monetization potential**: Higher CPC = more valuable audience

### Keyword Difficulty (KD)
- **0-20**: Very easy to rank for
- **21-40**: Easy to rank for
- **41-60**: Moderate difficulty
- **61-80**: Hard to rank for
- **81-100**: Very difficult to rank for

### Competition Level
- **Low**: Few competitors targeting this keyword
- **Medium**: Moderate competition
- **High**: Intense competition from established sites
- **Very High**: Dominated by large brands

## Interpreting the Results

### High Opportunity Keywords
Look for:
- **High volume** (1000+ monthly searches)
- **Low-Medium difficulty** (KD < 40)
- **Medium-High CPC** ($2+)

This indicates a valuable keyword with achievable ranking potential.

### Long-Tail Keywords
- Lower volume but often easier to rank for
- Less competition but more targeted audience
- Better conversion rates on average

### Commercial vs. Informational
- **High CPC** → Commercial intent (product/service queries)
- **Low CPC** → Informational intent (learning/research queries)

## Example Workflow

1. Analyze broad keyword: "project management"
2. Check difficulty and volume
3. If difficulty is high, look for related long-tail variations
4. Compare CPC to understand commercial value
5. Target the best opportunity (volume + feasibility balance)

## Tips

- **Language matters**: Different languages have different search volumes and competition levels
- **Check trends**: Seasonal keywords peak at specific times
- **Compare keywords**: Analyze multiple variations to find the sweet spot
- **Monitor over time**: Keyword metrics change as markets evolve
